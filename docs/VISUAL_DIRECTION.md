# VISUAL DIRECTION

**Agent 02: Senior Digital Art Director**

## 1. Core Concept
**Premium futuristic spatial technology.**
Digital architecture + interactive map + cinematic product experience.

## 2. Color Palette
- **Primary:** Near-black (`#0A0A0C` to `#050505`) - Defines the VOID and spatial depth.
- **Secondary:** Warm off-white (`#F8F9FA` to `#E5E7EB`) - For high-contrast, premium typography.
- **Support:** Soft gray (`#888888` to `#A1A1AA`) - For metadata, secondary text, and architectural edges.
- **Signal:** Electric blue (`#2563EB` / `rgb(37, 99, 235)`) - Used sparingly as a functional signal. It indicates connection, activation, data flow, and importance.

## 3. Typography
- **Primary Font:** `Inter` (or similar modern geometric sans-serif like `Geist` or `Satoshi`).
- **Characteristics:** Highly readable, strong weights for headlines, precise tracking, generous whitespace.
- **Usage:** Restrained labels, small precise metadata, rigorous alignment. Avoid oversized meaningless text.

## 4. Material Language (3D World)
- **Matte Black:** Base architecture, platforms, and structures. Absorbs light, creating a sense of mass.
- **Warm White:** Emissive labels, glowing surfaces, and important typographic markers within the 3D space.
- **Brushed Metal:** Used sparingly for structural joints or premium accents.
- **Glass:** Used ONLY functionally, not as a blanket aesthetic.
- **Electric Blue (Emissive):** Pure energy, signal lines, network connections, and activation states.

## 5. Lighting
- **Style:** Cinematic, Restrained, Atmospheric.
- **Components:**
  - Soft ambient lighting to reveal the geometry of the black structures.
  - Directional rim light to catch edges and provide scale.
  - Controlled blue emissive accents (bloom used very sparingly, avoiding "nightclub" aesthetics).

## 6. Interaction Language
- **Camera:** Moves deliberately based on scroll (inertia-driven).
- **Objects:** Subtle physical response to camera proximity and pointer hover.
- **Particles:** Respond to scroll momentum and pointer proximity.

## 7. Things to Avoid
- Generic SaaS illustrations.
- Cyberpunk / Neon city aesthetics.
- Cryptocurrency tropes.
- Excessive glassmorphism and gradients.
- Humanoid AI robots or "floating brains".
