**None**

## Limitations
- I cannot authenticate with Figma.
- I cannot read Figma URLs.
- I cannot inspect Figma components, spacing, typography, or colors directly from a Figma file.
- I cannot export SVGs or images from Figma.

## Recommendation
**E. Figma unavailable**

Since I cannot connect to Figma, the workflow must rely on alternative methods for design-to-code translation. You will need to manually provide design specifications (e.g., color hex codes, typography rules, layout dimensions, exported SVG/PNG assets) or describe the designs to me, and I will implement them directly in code.
